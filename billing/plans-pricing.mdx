## What every plan includes

All plans include the following at no extra cost:

- **Always-on DDoS protection** — traffic scrubbing is active 24/7 with no caps or add-on fees
- **AMD Ryzen 9 9950X CPU** — high single-thread performance, which is critical for Minecraft and similar games
- **DDR5 RAM** — faster memory speeds compared to older DDR4 hardware
- **Port allocations** — multiple ports so you can run query, RCON, and additional services alongside your main server

<Info>
There are no artificial player limits on any plan. Your server's RAM and CPU allocation are the only constraints. A Creeper plan with 2 GB of RAM will naturally support fewer players than a Wither plan with 12 GB, but Sculk Hosting does not impose a separate player cap on top of that.
</Info>

## Add-ons

**Dedicated IP addresses** are available as a paid add-on. A dedicated IP gives your server its own IP address instead of a shared IP with a port, which is useful if you want players to connect without specifying a port number. You can add a dedicated IP to any plan through the client area at [my.sculkhosting.com](https://my.sculkhosting.com).
