Distributed denial-of-service (DDoS) attacks flood your server with junk traffic in an attempt to knock it offline. In gaming, this means disconnected players, unplayable lag, or a server that won't respond at all. Sculk Hosting's enterprise-grade mitigation runs automatically in front of every server we provision — so you're protected the moment your server goes live, without any configuration on your part.

## Always-on protection

There is no toggle, no protection tier to upgrade, and no ticket to file. Mitigation is active on your server's IP address from the first second it's online.

<Info>
  You don't need to do anything to enable DDoS protection. It's included with every Sculk Hosting plan across all regions.
</Info>

## What "enterprise-grade mitigation" means for you

Enterprise-grade mitigation means your traffic passes through Sculk's scrubbing infrastructure before it ever reaches your game server. Malicious packets are identified and filtered out while legitimate player traffic continues through uninterrupted.

## What happens during an attack

When an attack hits, Sculk's mitigation layer identifies the abnormal traffic pattern and begins filtering it automatically. For the vast majority of attacks, this happens fast enough that your players experience no disruption.

<Note>
  DDoS protection cannot stop every attack. Very large or sustained attacks may cause brief disruption even with mitigation active. If your server becomes unreachable, check the service status page at [my.sculkhosting.com/status](https://my.sculkhosting.com/status) to see whether a network event is affecting your region.
</Note>

## Acceptable use

Sculk's infrastructure is shared across all customers. To keep the network healthy for everyone, the following are prohibited under our Acceptable Use Policy:

- Intentionally attracting DDoS attacks to your server or Sculk's network
- Running booter, stresser, or IP stresser services on your server
- Using any Sculk infrastructure to launch attacks against other networks or services

Violations may result in immediate suspension without notice.

<Warning>
  You may not use Sculk Hosting infrastructure to attack others or to deliberately draw attacks onto the network. See our Terms of Service for full details.
</Warning>

## If you're experiencing disruption

Unusual latency or repeated disconnections don't always mean an attack is in progress. Before contacting support, check a few things:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check the status page">
    Visit [my.sculkhosting.com/status](https://my.sculkhosting.com/status) to see whether there's a known network event affecting your server's region.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review your server console">
    Log in to [panel.sculkhosting.com](https://panel.sculkhosting.com) and open the console to check for server-side errors such as out-of-memory or crash loops.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Contact support">
    If the status page shows no issues and your server looks healthy, reach out to our team at [support@sculkhosting.com](mailto:support@sculkhosting.com) or join us on [Discord](https://discord.gg/tesjNBszQe). We have human support available 24/7.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Keep the status page bookmarked. It's the fastest way to tell whether an issue is on Sculk's end or local to your server.
</Tip>
