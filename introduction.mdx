Sculk Hosting is a game server hosting platform built from the ground up for players, communities, and server admins who need reliable, low-latency performance without the complexity of managing raw infrastructure. Whether you're running a small Minecraft server for friends or a large Rust or ARK community with hundreds of concurrent players, Sculk Hosting gives you a fully managed server you can spin up in minutes — backed by AMD Ryzen 9 9950X hardware, DDR5 RAM, NVMe storage, and enterprise-grade DDoS protection at every tier.

## What is Sculk Hosting?

Sculk Hosting is a software-as-a-service (SaaS) game server hosting provider. You pay for a plan, Sculk provisions your server automatically, and you manage everything through a clean web-based control panel. There is no bare-metal provisioning, no OS configuration, and no networking setup required on your end.

All servers run on AMD Ryzen 9 9950X processors — one of the fastest consumer-grade CPUs available — paired with DDR5 RAM and NVMe SSD storage. This hardware combination ensures low tick latency and fast world loading across every supported game.

## Who is Sculk Hosting for?

Sculk Hosting is designed for:

- **Gamers** who want a private server for their friend group without dealing with self-hosting complexity.
- **Community managers** who run public servers and need stable uptime and room to grow.
- **Server admins** who want full control over game configuration, mods, and plugins through a capable panel.
