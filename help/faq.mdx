Whether you're evaluating Sculk Hosting for the first time or already running a server, the questions below cover the topics customers ask most often. If you don't find what you're looking for, see the note at the bottom of this page.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What performance can I expect from Sculk?">
    Sculk is powered by high-frequency AMD Ryzen 9950X CPUs and DDR5 RAM, ensuring your server handles heavy mods and high player counts with zero lag.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does Sculk support dedicated IP addresses?">
    Yes, dedicated IPs are available as an add-on. This allows you to use the default port (e.g., 25565 for Minecraft) without needing a port suffix.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I invite people to co-manage my server?">
    Absolutely. Our panel includes a sub-user system where you can grant specific permissions to friends or staff members.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I switch my server's game at any time?">
    Yes! You can use our one-click installer to switch between different games or versions instantly without losing your files.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Will my server have any player limits?">
    We do not impose artificial player limits. Your only limit is the hardware resources (RAM/CPU) allocated to your plan.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How can I manage my server's files?">
    You can manage files directly through our web-based File Manager or via SFTP for bulk uploads and external editing.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How much storage does my plan have?">
    All plans include unlimited NVMe storage with fair use. Your plan comes with a guaranteed storage allocation (varies by plan), and you can use more as long as it's reasonable and doesn't affect other tenants.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What games can I host?">
    Sculk supports 13+ games including Minecraft, Rust, CS2, ARK, Palworld, Arma 3, Squad, 7 Days to Die, Satisfactory, Conan Exiles, Garry's Mod, Hytale, and TeamSpeak. Use the one-click installer in the panel to deploy any supported game in seconds.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I contact support?">
    Submit a ticket at [my.sculkhosting.com/tickets](https://my.sculkhosting.com/tickets) or email [support@sculkhosting.com](mailto:support@sculkhosting.com). Support is available 24/7 and staffed by humans, not bots.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Where are the servers located?">
    Sculk Hosting operates nodes in New York, Los Angeles, Frankfurt, Paris, Singapore, Sydney, and Tokyo. You can select your preferred region when deploying a new server from the client area.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
