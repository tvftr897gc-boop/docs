Sculk Hosting offers five plans across three performance tiers, named after Minecraft-themed items and mobs. Each tier is tuned for a different type of workload — from small friend-group servers to large communities running resource-heavy games. All plans run on the same AMD Ryzen 9 9950X hardware with DDR5 RAM and NVMe SSD storage, so the difference between tiers is strictly the amount of vCores and RAM allocated to your server.

## Plan tiers

Plans are grouped into three tiers based on use case:

- **Azure tier** — Entry-level plans (Creeper, Zombie) suited for lighter games and smaller communities.
- **Sapphire tier** — Mid-range plans (Vex, Wither) for popular multiplayer games with moderate to high player counts.
- **Abyss tier** — High-performance plans (Elytra) for large communities running demanding titles like ARK, Squad, or Arma 3.

## What every plan includes

Regardless of which plan you choose, you get:

- Unlimited NVMe SSD storage
- Always-on, enterprise-grade DDoS protection
- Access to every supported game via the one-click installer
- Full control panel access at [panel.sculkhosting.com](https://panel.sculkhosting.com)
- Server locations across New York, Los Angeles, Frankfurt, Paris, Singapore, Sydney, and Tokyo

## Billing cycle discounts

You can reduce your monthly cost by committing to a longer billing cycle:

| Cycle | Discount |
| --- | --- |
| Monthly | Full price |
| Quarterly (every 3 months) | 5% off |
| Yearly | 15% off |

Selecting a longer cycle locks in the discounted rate for the full period. You can choose your billing cycle during checkout.
