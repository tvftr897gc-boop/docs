Before you can order a game server, you need a Sculk Hosting account. Registration takes under two minutes and gives you access to the client billing area at [my.sculkhosting.com](https://my.sculkhosting.com), where you manage subscriptions, invoices, and support tickets. Follow the steps below to get set up.

<Note>
  Use the discount code **BLABLA** at checkout to get 10% off your first order. You'll enter this code during the payment step after completing registration.
</Note>

## Registration steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to the client area and click Register">
    Open [my.sculkhosting.com](https://my.sculkhosting.com) in your browser. On the login page, click the **Register** link to open the account creation form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your name, email, and password">
    Fill in your first name, last name, email address, and a strong password. Your email address is used for login, invoices, and service notifications, so use one you check regularly.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify your email address">
    After submitting the registration form, Sculk Hosting sends a verification email to the address you provided. Open the email and click the verification link to confirm your account. Check your spam folder if the email does not arrive within a few minutes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Log in to the client area">
    Once your email is verified, return to [my.sculkhosting.com](https://my.sculkhosting.com) and log in with your email and password. You now have full access to your billing area, where you can order services, view invoices, and open support tickets.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Keep your billing email accessible after signup. Sculk Hosting sends invoice reminders and renewal notices to this address. If you miss a payment, your server may be suspended.
</Tip>

## What's next?

With your account created, you're ready to choose a plan and launch your first server. Head to [Choosing the right game server plan](/getting-started/choose-a-plan) to compare options, or jump straight to the [quick start guide](/getting-started/quick-start) if you already know what you need.
