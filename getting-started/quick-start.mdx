Once you have a Sculk Hosting account, you can go from checkout to a live, connectable game server in just a few minutes. This guide walks you through the entire process — placing your order, accessing the game panel, installing a game, and getting your server IP so you and your players can connect.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in to the client area">
    Go to [my.sculkhosting.com](https://my.sculkhosting.com) and sign in with your email and password.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your plan">
    Browse the available plans and select the one that fits your game and player count. If you're unsure which plan to pick, see the [plan comparison guide](/getting-started/choose-a-plan) for a full breakdown by resource and recommended game.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select your server location and billing cycle">
    During the order process, choose the server location closest to your players. Available locations are: New York, Los Angeles, Frankfurt, Paris, Singapore, Sydney, and Tokyo. Then choose your billing cycle — monthly, quarterly (5% off), or yearly (15% off).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete checkout">
    Review your order and enter your payment details. At the promo code field, enter **BLABLA** to get 10% off your first order. Submit your payment to confirm the order.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for instant provisioning">
    Sculk Hosting provisions your server automatically within seconds of payment confirmation. You do not need to take any action — your server will be ready by the time you open the panel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the game panel">
    Go to [panel.sculkhosting.com](https://panel.sculkhosting.com) and log in. You'll see your newly provisioned server listed on the dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a game using the one-click installer">
    Click on your server, then open the **Startup** or **Game Installer** section. Choose the game you want to run from the supported game list and confirm the installation. The panel will download and configure the game server automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start your server">
    Once installation is complete, go to the **Console** tab and click **Start**. Watch the console output to confirm the server has started successfully.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect with your server's IP and port">
    Your server's IP address and port are displayed on the panel overview page. Share these with your players so they can connect from their game client.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Your server IP and port are always visible on the panel overview page for your server. Return there any time you need the connection details.
</Note>

<Tip>
  You can switch games at any time using the one-click installer without losing your existing server files. Previous game data is preserved so you can switch back later.
</Tip>
